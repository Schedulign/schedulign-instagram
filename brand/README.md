# The Schedulign logo

Chosen by Sam on September 25, 2026: Slot, the calendar-tile character, replaces the clock-handshake-cursor logo. The concepts and all three rounds are on the canvas "Schedulign Logo Concepts" (claude.ai/artifact/HGtUPLQCQG4wwb66jAWkH8); round 3 is the final set.

## The family

| Use | Mark | File |
|---|---|---|
| App icon, social avatar, anywhere Slot appears alone | **The tile** (round 2, "B2"): lime tile, navy top band, binder rings standing above, open eyes, smile | `slot-tile.svg` |
| Favicon and anything under about 24 px | The tile reduced to band and eyes | `slot-favicon.svg` |
| Anything that means "booked": confirmations, success screens, celebration posts | **Booked** (round 3, "C3"): happy closed eyes, a check mark for a smile | `slot-booked.svg` |
| Site header, the main logo | Slot standing on the **i** as its dot, waving, over the wordmark | Canvas, round 3, lockup 1 |
| Banners, the website hero, merch | Slot **sitting on the h**, legs dangling, one hand holding on | Canvas, round 3, lockup 2 |
| A booking confirmed | The i-dot Slot with both arms up and the booked face | Canvas, round 3, lockup 3 |

The wordmark is "schedulign" in lowercase Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold (800), letter-spacing −0.02em, navy. The lockups are live text on the canvas; turn them into outlined SVGs before they go into the product or print.

## Colors

- Lime `#b9d32c`: Slot's body. Lime means the booking page is at work.
- Navy `#303159`: the top band, the wordmark, the check mark.
- Ink `#1f2238`: eyes and smile.
- On a navy background, the top band turns white so it stays visible.

## Rules

- The check-mark smile means booked and nothing else.
- Never stretch the tile or recolor Slot.
- The product (schedulign.com) still shows the old logo until that change is made in the product repo.
